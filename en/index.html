HIDRA forecast

Ensemble sea-level forecast of the HIDRA[1] model for the Koper Mareographic Station. The most recent forecast is shown. To analyse HIDRA in different conditions, older forecasts can also be selected using the slider. The measured sea-level is shown in green and HIDRA predictions are colored blue. The warning sea-level values are also shown as yellow, orange and red lines. HIDRA considers 24 hours of the past sea-level measurements in its forecast. The range of the measurements used as input is denoted as a grayed out area.

About HIDRA

HIDRA is a deep model for sea-level forecasting and is the result of a collaboration between Visual Cognitive Systems Lab, University of Ljubljana, Faculty of Computer and Information Science and Slovenian Environment Agency (ARSO). HIDRA bases its predicitons on ensemble weather forecasts of the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) and past 24 hours of sea-level measurements at the Koper Mareographic Station (source: ARSO). The model was trained on a dataset of ECMWF weather forecasts for the years 2006-2016 and Koper sea-level measurements for the same time period.
